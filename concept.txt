A menu-driven incremental where you rise from a nobody gambler to running a global gambling empire. Everything happens through clean UI panels—no 3D world—just layered systems, stats, and decisions.

Core Loop
Start with a small amount of chips
Play minigames (blackjack, slots, etc.)
Earn money + unlock modifiers
Invest into passive systems (AI gamblers, auto tables)
Expand into new games and mechanics
Eventually become the house, not the player
Main Systems
🎰 Casino Games (Active Gameplay)

Each game is its own UI tab with depth, not just RNG spam.

Slots
Reel upgrades, symbol weighting, jackpot pools
“Rigging” mechanics (legally questionable upgrades later on)
Blackjack
Card counting system (visible stat progression)
Unlockable decision assists → eventually automated perfect play
Poker (PvE-style)
Play against AI personalities with traits
Bluff mechanic as a timed UI minigame
Build a “playstyle” (aggressive, passive, unpredictable)
Roulette
Probability manipulation upgrades
Pattern tracking / bias detection systems
Chess (wildcard minigame)
Betting on outcomes rather than playing directly
Upgrade prediction accuracy
Eventually simulate matches instantly
📈 Incremental Layer (The Real Game)

This is where it becomes more than a casino simulator.

1. Player Stats
Luck (affects RNG subtly)
Intelligence (better decisions / odds visibility)
Nerve (risk bonuses)
Reputation (unlocks higher stakes tables)
2. Passive Income
Hire NPC gamblers → they play games for you
Upgrade their skill + risk tolerance
Assign them to different games
3. Automation
Auto-spin slots
Auto-play blackjack with strategy profiles
Fully simulated poker tables generating income
🧠 Meta Progression
Prestige System: “Cash Out”
Reset progress for permanent bonuses
Gain:
“Influence”
“Insider Info” (lets you bend odds slightly)
Corruption System (Late Game)

You stop playing fair.

Bribe dealers
Adjust RNG behind the scenes
Skim profits from NPC gamblers
Risk: getting “investigated” (temporary debuffs/events)
🏢 Becoming the House

Mid–late game twist:

You stop being the gambler
You run the casino

New mechanics:

Set house edge
Design games
Attract whales (high-risk NPCs)
Manage risk vs profit
🎲 Events & Minigame Variants
Daily tournaments (UI bracket system)
High-roller tables (big risk, huge reward)
Limited-time modes
“All Luck Mode” (pure RNG chaos)
“Skill Only Mode” (no randomness, just decision making)
🧩 Collection Systems

Fits your style of “index” mechanics:

Card decks (modifiers)
Slot symbols
Poker opponents
Special “rigging techniques”

Completing sets gives permanent boosts.

⚖️ Interesting Design Twist

Instead of just “more money = better,” introduce:

Risk Curve

Playing safer slows progression heavily
Playing risky accelerates but can wipe chunks of progress

So players constantly decide:

Do I grind safely, or gamble hard for a spike?

Casino games naturally fit menus

No need for animations → faster gameplay loops
Easy to scale complexity without visual clutter
